MoE caveat

RAG Plumbing

Embedders + Rerankers · the retrieval layer

Two-stage retrieval: an embedder turns chunks into vectors and pulls the top 50–100 candidates fast; a reranker (cross-encoder) re-scores those survivors against the query for +15–40% precision. These models are small — CPU to a few GB — so pick by dimensions, context, language, and license, not VRAM tier.

TWO TRUTHS: the retriever sets the ceiling — a reranker can't surface what recall already missed, so fix embeddings/chunking first. And reranker size ≠ quality; start small and only scale up if your golden-set eval justifies it.
NOTE: rerankers aren't part of Ollama's embedding API — serve them with Hugging Face TEI, Infinity, or FlagEmbedding alongside your Ollama stack.

VRAM rule of thumbAt Q4 figure roughly 0.6 GB × billions of params for weights, then add KV-cache for your context window. "Memory" means GPU VRAM or Apple unified memory.

Why these picksHeroes favour real Ollama / llama.cpp availability and reliable tool-calling, not just leaderboard rank. Dense models tend to beat MoE on agent-loop reliability at the same size.

This moves fastThe open-weight field shifts monthly — new GLM, Kimi, DeepSeek, Qwen and Llama point-releases land constantly. Treat this as a June 2026 snapshot and confirm params, license and context on the live model card.
